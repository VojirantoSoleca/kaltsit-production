# Kal'tsit natural gaze mechanics

The tiny torso, belt, hands, left thigh straps and both feet stay anchored in a frontal planted pose. The large separate head turns in shallow yaw and pitch; feline ears and bob haircut follow the head as one coherent volume. No whole-character rotation or leaning to fake directions. Keep the same broad green irises, dark rims, pale highlights and sleepy eyelids. Rotate the eyes naturally in their sockets with the head, without replacement pupils or exaggerated googly eyes. The face remains visible at all directions.

Up: chin/nose lift, eyes clearly toward the top edge, more lower-face exposure; never neutral straight ahead.
Right: shallow head turn to viewer right with nose right of head center and irises toward screen right; body remains frontal.
Down: chin tucks, gaze clearly toward bottom edge, upper lids lower, more top of head showing; never merely eyes closed.
Left: shallow head turn to viewer left with nose left of head center and irises toward screen left.

Diagonals combine both axes in even, continuous 22.5-degree steps. Keep head size, haircut, ear size, green eyes, expression and overall scale consistent. The collar/neck connection stays physical. Maintain asymmetry of the anatomical left leg strap. Do not rotate to profile or back view. Do not shift the feet. Direction loop is clockwise from up: upper right, right, lower right, down, lower left, left, upper left, up.
